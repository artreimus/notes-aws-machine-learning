## Lifecycle Caveats

- Amazon Lookout for Metrics and Amazon Lookout for Vision appear in some certification service lists, but AWS General Reference lists them in full shutdown.
- Amazon Fraud Detector and Amazon Lookout for Equipment are sunset/no-new-customer services; keep only lifecycle-aware study notes.
- Amazon QLDB is in full shutdown and is not a primary MLA-C01 target.
